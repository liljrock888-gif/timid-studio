Custom orders

How a custom project works

Have something in mind? Gifts, matching sets, event favors or a total one-off. Here's the process.

  1. Share your idea

    Tell me what you want made, plus any photos, colors or sizes.

  2. Get a quote

    I'll reply with a price, yarn options and a timeline.

  3. I make it

    Your piece is stitched by hand, with progress pics along the way.

  4. It's yours

    Finished, checked and packed up with care.
